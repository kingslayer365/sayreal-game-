🎮 SAYREAL – Your Imagination, Your World!

What if you could type anything and bring it to life inside a 2D world?

Welcome to SAYREAL, a creative 2D sandbox game where imagination has no limits!

In SAYREAL, you don't just play the game — you create the experience yourself. Type anything you imagine and watch it become part of your world. Create objects, characters, and ideas, then interact with them in exciting ways.

✨ Features

- 🧠 Create Anything: Type your imagination and turn your ideas into game elements.
- 🔗 Connect Anything: Link objects and bring your creations together.
- 📎 Attach & Interact: Attach creations and experiment with how they work.
- ⚔️ Fight & Battle: Make your creations fight and experience your own battles.
- 🌍 Endless Creativity: Explore different possibilities and create unique experiences.
- 🎮 Your Rules: Experiment, build, connect, and play your own way.

SAYREAL is more than a normal 2D game. It's a world where your imagination controls the possibilities.

You type it. You create it. You control it.
